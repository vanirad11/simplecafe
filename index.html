Our Story

A Simple Dream for Great Coffee

Simple Café began with a very simple idea: create a quiet, welcoming place where people could enjoy genuinely good food and coffee without pretense. Founded by a family of passionate food lovers, our doors opened with a mission to bring people together over warmth and quality.

Every ingredient we serve is chosen with intent. From ethically sourced beans roasted locally to bread baked every morning nearby, we keep our food simple, fresh, and wholesome.
